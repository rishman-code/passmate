Privacy Policy

PassMate  ·  Last updated: 1 August 2026

Short version: PassMate does not collect your name, email address, location, or any personal information. Your practice progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

1. Who we are

PassMate is an iOS app that helps learners prepare for the UK DVSA driving theory test. It is developed and operated as an independent product. PassMate is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA).

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: rohan.shinn@hotmail.com

2. What data PassMate collects

PassMate collects as little data as possible. Here is the full picture:

Practice progress (stored on your device only)
Your answers, scores, and session history are stored in the app's memory while the app is open. This data is never uploaded to any server, never tied to an account, and is cleared when you delete the app. We do not have access to your practice history.

Purchase status (via RevenueCat)
If you purchase the premium upgrade, RevenueCat — our payment processor — verifies your purchase using an anonymous device identifier. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Question and content delivery (via Supabase)
The app fetches questions from a database hosted by Supabase. These are standard HTTPS requests; Supabase's servers may log your IP address as part of normal server operation, as any web server does. No account is created and no personal data is associated with these requests. See Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

AI explanations (pre-generated)
The AI explanations shown in the app were generated in advance using Anthropic's Claude API and are stored in our database. In normal use, no data from your session is sent to Anthropic — you receive a pre-written explanation from our cache, not a live API call. Anthropic's privacy policy is at anthropic.com/privacy.

3. What PassMate does NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Device identifiers for advertising (IDFA)
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data
  • Crash reports or analytics (no analytics SDK is installed)

4. Cookies and tracking

PassMate is a native iOS app. It does not use cookies. It does not contain any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs (such as Firebase or Mixpanel), or cross-app tracking technology. Your App Tracking Transparency permission is not requested because we do not track you across apps or websites.

5. Children's privacy

PassMate is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal data is collected at all, PassMate complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because PassMate does not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing to delete from our end. Your on-device practice history is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app. If you made a purchase, RevenueCat retains anonymous purchase records as required for receipt validation — contact RevenueCat directly if you wish to request deletion of those records.

7. Security

All communication between the app and our database (Supabase) uses HTTPS encryption. We do not store passwords or payment card details — purchases are handled entirely by Apple and RevenueCat.
