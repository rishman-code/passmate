Privacy Policy

GreenLight  ·  Last updated: 3 September 2026

Short version: GreenLight offers a free account (email and password, or Sign in with Apple) so your progress syncs across devices — or you can use Guest Mode, which keeps everything on your device only. If you have an account, we store your email address and your practice/test data against it. Subscriptions are processed by RevenueCat and the App Store. We never sell your data, and you can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from inside the app.

1. Who we are

GreenLight is an iOS app that helps learners prepare for the UK DVSA driving theory test. It is developed and operated as an independent product. GreenLight is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA).

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: sales@alphaalife.com

2. What data GreenLight collects

What we collect depends on whether you create an account:

Account information (if you sign up)
Creating an account requires an email address and a password (or Sign in with Apple, where available). Your password is hashed and managed by Supabase Auth — we never see or store it in plain text. Your account lets your progress sync across devices.

Guest Mode (if you don't sign up)
You can use GreenLight without creating an account. In Guest Mode, your practice progress stays only on your device and is never sent to our servers. Guest data is not linked to any account and is cleared when you delete the app.

Practice and test data (if you have an account)
Your answered questions, mock test scores, category performance, streaks, flagged questions to revisit, and your driving-test journey status (e.g. a booked test date or pass/fail outcome) are stored against your account in our Supabase database, so this data syncs across your devices and survives a reinstall.

Subscription and purchase status (via RevenueCat and the App Store)
Subscriptions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We receive your subscription entitlement status (active or not) linked to your account so premium features unlock correctly — we never see or store your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Question and content delivery (via Supabase)
The app fetches questions from a database hosted by Supabase over standard HTTPS requests; Supabase's servers may log your IP address as part of normal server operation, as any web server does. See Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

Device notifications (if enabled)
If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders (e.g. to come back and practice, or before your test certificate expires) directly on your device. These are scheduled locally by the app itself — they are not sent from our servers, and we don't track whether you open them.

AI explanations (mostly pre-generated)
The AI explanations shown in the app were generated in advance using Anthropic's Claude API and are stored in our database. Fetching a cached explanation is an authenticated request tied to your account, but in normal use no data about your specific answers is sent to Anthropic — you receive a pre-written explanation from our cache, not a live API call. Anthropic's privacy policy is at anthropic.com/privacy.

3. What GreenLight does NOT collect

  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Device identifiers for advertising (IDFA)
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data
  • Crash reports or analytics (no analytics SDK is installed)
  • Your payment card or bank details (handled entirely by Apple and RevenueCat)

4. Cookies and tracking

GreenLight is a native iOS app. It does not use cookies. It does not contain any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs (such as Firebase or Mixpanel), or cross-app tracking technology. Your App Tracking Transparency permission is not requested because we do not track you across apps or websites.

5. Children's privacy

GreenLight is rated 4+ by the App Store. Creating an account requires an email address, so we recommend account creation only be done by, or with the involvement of, a parent or guardian for younger users — Guest Mode, which requires no email address or account, is available for anyone who prefers not to sign up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if we become aware that we have, we will delete the associated account and its data.

6. Data retention and deletion

If you have an account, your data (email, progress, test history, journey status) is retained for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app — this immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data from our database, and cannot be undone. Guest-mode data lives only on your device and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app. If you made a purchase, RevenueCat and Apple retain records as required for receipt validation and legal/tax purposes even after account deletion — contact RevenueCat or Apple directly to request deletion of those records.

7. Security

All communication between the app and our services (Supabase, RevenueCat) uses HTTPS encryption. Your password is hashed and managed by Supabase Auth — we never see or store your plain-text password. We do not store your payment card details; purchases are handled entirely by Apple and RevenueCat.

  • Apple App Store — app distribution, payment processing, and (optionally) Sign in with Apple. Privacy policy →
