Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new one. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

I want to delete my account and data

Open the app, go to Profile, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and everything tied to it, and can't be undone.

My progress isn't syncing between devices

Progress only syncs if you're signed in with an account on both devices. If you're using Guest Mode, your progress stays on that one device only — sign up for a free account to sync it.

Something else

Email us at sales@alphaalife.com and describe the issue — screenshots help if it's something visual.
